The purpose of the module is to provide developers with the knowledge and skills to design and implement a data persistence layer for an application. During the training, developers will learn about the different types of database management systems (DBMSs), including SQL (relational databases) and NoSQL (non-relational databases), and the advantages and disadvantages of each. They will also learn how to design a database schema that meets the needs of the application and how to choose the appropriate DBMS for the job.
